## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Titanic Escape Simulator™**. This trainer enhances the investigation and survival experience aboard the Titanic. It provides control over stamina, cold exposure, flooding, environmental hazards, locked areas, stealth pressure, and access to important clues while preserving the game's exploration, puzzle-solving, and choice-driven structure.

**Target Audience:** Recommended for players who want to focus on the mystery, ship exploration, environmental puzzles, historical atmosphere, alternate choices, and multiple endings without being limited by stamina, cold, flooding, stealth threats, or physical hazards.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Stamina** — Prevents stamina depletion during climbing, movement, and other physically demanding survival actions.
- **Hazard Protection** — Reduces the impact of physical hazards, blocked routes, tilting corridors, and dangerous environmental events.
- **Stealth Protection** — Reduces enemy awareness while entering forbidden areas and investigating hidden parts of the ship.

### 📌 Survival

- **Cold Resistance** — Reduces or disables the effects of freezing conditions during the sinking sequence.
- **Flooding Control** — Pauses or adjusts the rising-water progression so flooded areas can be explored more safely.

### 📌 Investigation

- **Evidence Assistance** — Helps locate and collect investigation items, clues, and evidence connected to the shipboard mystery.

### 📌 Puzzle

- **Door and Lock Access** — Provides easier access to locked rooms and restricted sections without removing the game's exploration objectives.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Safe Exploration** — Makes it easier to examine public rooms, service corridors, cargo spaces, machinery areas, and lifeboat decks.

### 📌 Story

- **Choice-Friendly Progress** — Supports repeated playthroughs by making it easier to explore alternative routes and experience different endings.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Enhancement** — The trainer does not add weapons or create a separate combat system; its threat-related options support avoidance and survival.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer's stamina and hazard options when exploring steep, damaged, or flooded sections of the ship. Unlimited stamina is useful during emergency routes, while hazard protection helps when corridors tilt, paths become blocked, and the environment changes during the sinking.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect evidence, clues, and tools normally as you investigate cabins, crime scenes, locked rooms, and restricted decks. Door and lock assistance can shorten access puzzles, but careful item collection remains useful for following the mystery and progressing through the investigation.

### World & Environment

Explore the Titanic before and during the disaster, including luxury public rooms, service corridors, cargo areas, machinery spaces, and lifeboat decks. Flooding control and cold resistance are most useful after the iceberg strike, when rising water, failing lights, blocked routes, and freezing conditions reshape the available paths.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is not the central gameplay system. Threats are handled through stealth, avoidance, environmental awareness, and survival decisions. Use stealth protection to investigate forbidden areas with less risk, while hazard protection helps during dangerous escape sequences.
